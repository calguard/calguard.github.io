Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  |  SmartScan Version 1.0.0

Introduction

SmartScan ("we," "our," or "us") is a document scanner app that helps you digitize, organize, and search your documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information.

Free Plan

  • 5 scans per day
  • Ads (App Open + Rewarded)
  • On-device OCR for search
  • Local file management

Premium Plan

  • Unlimited scans
  • Ad-free experience
  • Google Drive cloud sync
  • Full-text search (FTS)
  • Office document OCR

Information We Collect

Camera & Documents:

  • SmartScan uses your device camera to scan documents.
  • All scans are processed locally on your device.
  • We do not upload your documents to our servers.

OCR & Text Search:

  • On-device OCR via ML Kit to extract text from scans.
  • Extracted text stored locally in an encrypted database.
  • You can delete OCR data at any time.

Google Drive Sync (Optional):

  • Sign in with Google to sync scans to your Drive.
  • Stored in a "SmartScan" folder in your Google Drive.
  • Disconnect at any time from Settings.

Advertising (Free Plan)

  • Free users see App Open ads on cold start and Rewarded ads for bonus scans.
  • Ads served by Google AdMob — may use Advertising ID for personalization.
  • EEA, UK, and Brazil users receive non-personalized ads by default.
  • Children receive only child-appropriate ads via tagForChildDirectedTreatment.
  • Premium users see no ads.

Children's Privacy

  • We comply with COPPA. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
  • Child-directed ads served via tagForChildDirectedTreatment.
  • No personalized ads for children.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal information with third parties. All data is stored locally on your device or your own Google Drive.

Your Rights

  • Access, delete, or export your data anytime.
  • Disconnect Google Drive or disable notifications in Settings.
