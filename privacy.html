Privacy Policy

In Plain Words

SmartScan is a document scanner. It turns paper documents into digital files on your phone. This page explains what we do with your information — using everyday words, not confusing legal talk.

What We See

Your documents. Your scans stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers. We cannot see them.

Text inside your scans. The app reads text from your scans so you can search them. This happens on your device, and the text is stored only on your phone.

Your Google account (only if you sign in). When you sign in with Google, we see your name, email address, and profile picture so we can show them to you. We do not store or share anything else from your Google account.

Camera. We use the camera only when you scan a document. We never record or store anything you don't scan.

Google Drive Sync (Optional)

If you turn on Cloud Sync, your scans are saved to a folder named "SmartScan" inside your own Google Drive account. We can only see and manage that folder. We cannot see the rest of your Drive.

You can turn this off or sign out anytime from Settings.

Ads

The free version of SmartScan shows ads to help keep it free. These ads are provided by Google (AdMob).

What this means for you:

  • Your scans, files, and personal documents are never used for ads.
  • Advertisers do not get your personal information.
  • We follow kid-safe ad rules, so we don't show personalized ads to kids.
  • If you buy Premium, ads are removed completely.

App Usage & Crash Help

We use Google's tools to see how many people use the app and to catch crashes when the app stops working. This helps us fix problems and make the app better.

This information is anonymous — we cannot tell who you are from it. It does not include your scans, documents, or personal files.

Notifications

The app can send you alerts (like "waiting for WiFi to sync") and helpful tips. You can turn these off anytime in Settings.

What We Never Do

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We never read or upload your documents.
  • We never share your scans or personal documents with other companies.

Your Controls

  • Delete your scans and search text anytime from the Files tab.
  • Turn off Google Drive sync or sign out of Google anytime.
  • Turn off notifications anytime in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Kids

SmartScan is made for everyone aged 13 and up. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including kids. Ads shown to kids are always kid-appropriate and not personalized.
