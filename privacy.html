Privacy Policy

Introduction

SmartScan ("we," "our," or "us") is a document scanner app that helps you digitize, organize, and search your documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information.

Information We Collect

Camera & Documents:

  • SmartScan uses your device camera to scan documents.
  • All scans are processed locally on your device.
  • We do not upload your documents to our servers.

OCR & Text Search:

  • On-device OCR via ML Kit to extract text from scans.
  • Extracted text stored locally in an encrypted database.
  • You can delete OCR data at any time.

Google Drive Sync (Optional):

  • Sign in with Google to sync scans to your Drive.
  • Stored in a "SmartScan" folder in your Google Drive.
  • Disconnect at any time from Settings.

Notifications

SmartScan uses on-device notifications for sync alerts, waiting-for-WiFi reminders, and app tips. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in Settings. No personal data is transmitted for notification purposes.

How We Use Information

We use your information only to provide the app (process, display, and search scanned documents) and to sync to Google Drive when enabled. We do not collect usage data or analytics.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal information with third parties. All data is stored locally or on your own Google Drive.

Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time.
  • Delete your data (local files, OCR index, or Google Drive files).
  • Disconnect Google Drive sync in Settings.
  • Disable notifications at any time.
