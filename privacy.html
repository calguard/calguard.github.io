Privacy Policy for SmartScan

Effective Date: March 2026

Introduction

SmartScan ("the App") is a document scanning application developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Camera and Storage

The App requires camera access to scan documents and storage access to save scanned PDF files locally. Images captured are processed on-device and are not uploaded to any server unless you choose to sync via Google Drive.

Google Drive Integration (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App requests access to your Google Drive to upload scanned documents. This is entirely optional. The App uses the DRIVE_FILE scope, meaning it can only access files it creates. We do not read, modify, or access any other files in your Google Drive.

Usage Data

The App does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics. All processing is done locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers except your explicit Google Drive sync.

How We Use Your Information

  • Camera access is used solely for real-time document scanning within the App.
  • Storage access is used to save scanned PDF files to your device.
  • Google Drive integration is used only to upload documents you explicitly choose to sync.

Data Storage and Security

All scanned documents are stored locally on your device in the app-private directory. If you enable Google Drive sync, documents are stored in your personal Google Drive under the App's designated folder. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: For ML Kit Document Scanner and Text Recognition (on-device processing).
  • Google Drive API: For optional cloud backup (only with your explicit consent).
  • Google Sign-In: For optional authentication (only with your explicit consent).

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of any changes within the App.

Contact Us
