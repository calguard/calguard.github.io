Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 | SmartScan Version 1.0.0

Introduction

SmartScan ("we," "our," or "us") is a document scanner app that helps you digitize, organize, and search your documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information.

Information We Collect

Camera & Documents:

  • SmartScan uses your device camera to scan documents.
  • All scans are processed locally on your device.
  • We do not upload your documents to our servers.

OCR & Text Search:

  • SmartScan performs on-device optical character recognition (OCR) using ML Kit to extract text from your scanned documents.
  • Extracted text is stored locally in an encrypted database and used only for in-app search functionality.
  • You can delete all OCR data at any time by clearing app data or deleting individual scans.

Google Drive Sync (Optional):

  • When you sign in with Google, you can sync your scans to your Google Drive.
  • Files are stored in a "SmartScan" folder in your Google Drive.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Settings.

Local Storage:

  • All scans and extracted text are stored locally on your device by default.
  • You can delete any scan or its associated OCR data at any time.

Notifications

SmartScan uses on-device notifications to keep you informed:

  • Cloud sync completion and failure alerts.
  • Waiting-for-WiFi reminders when auto-sync is paused.
  • App tips and tricks to help you use the app.

Notifications are entirely optional and can be disabled in Settings. No personal data is transmitted to any server for notification purposes.

How We Use Information

  • Provide the app: Process, display, and search scanned documents.
  • Sync to Drive: When enabled, upload scans to your Google Drive.
  • Improve the app: We do not collect any usage data or analytics. All logging is local and used only for debugging purposes.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal information with third parties. All data is stored locally on your device or on your own Google Drive when you choose to sync.

Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time.
  • Delete your data (local files, OCR index, or Google Drive files).
  • Disconnect Google Drive sync in Settings.
  • Disable notifications at any time.
