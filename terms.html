2. What SmartScan Does

SmartScan is a document scanner. It lets you:

  • Scan paper documents using your camera.
  • Read the text inside scans so you can search them.
  • Save and organize your scans on your phone.
  • Optionally back up your scans to your own Google Drive.

3. Your Documents Belong to You

Everything you scan stays on your device. Your documents are yours — we don't read them, upload them, or share them with anyone.

4. Google Drive Sync (Optional)

  • Cloud Sync is optional — turn it on only if you want your scans backed up to Google Drive.
  • Scans go to a folder named "SmartScan" in your own Drive. We can't see your other files.
  • You can disconnect or sign out anytime from Settings.
  • Keep your Google password safe. You're responsible for the security of your own account.

5. Reading Text & Search

  • The app reads text from your scans on your device — nothing is sent over the internet.
  • You can re-index (re-read) your scans anytime from the storage chip in the Files tab.
  • Deleting a scan also deletes its stored text.
